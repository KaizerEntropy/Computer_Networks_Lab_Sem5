- Switch to Router: Copper Straight-Through Cable (DCE to DTE).

5. PACKET LIFECYCLE & FORWARDING WALKTHROUGH:
Step 1: Host-M1 compares destination IP 192.168.149.2 with its subnet 192.168.148.0/24.
        Since it is on a remote subnet, Host-M1 must forward the packet to its Default
        Gateway: 192.168.148.1.
Step 2: Host-M1 resolves Router-JU-Main Fa0/0 MAC via ARP and transmits an Ethernet frame
        encapsulating the IP packet (Src IP: 192.168.148.2, Dst IP: 192.168.149.2).
Step 3: PC-LAB1-Switch switches the frame to port Fa0/24.
Step 4: Router-JU-Main receives frame, strips Ethernet header, inspects Dst IP 192.168.149.2.
Step 5: Router-JU-Main consults routing table; matches static route "192.168.149.0/24 via 192.168.150.2".
Step 6: Router-JU-Main decrements TTL (128 -> 127), recomputes IPv4 checksum.
Step 7: Router-JU-Main resolves next-hop 192.168.150.2 MAC and transmits across WAN link on Fa0/1.
Step 8: Router-JU-SL receives frame on Fa0/1, decrements TTL (127 -> 126), matches connected route
        "192.168.149.0/24 directly connected to Fa0/0".
Step 9: Router-JU-SL broadcasts ARP for 192.168.149.2 (causing the 1st packet timeout if cold),
        encapsulates into Ethernet frame with Host-S1 MAC as destination, and forwards via Fa0/0.
Step 10: Host-S1 receives ICMP Echo Request and generates ICMP Echo Reply back through its Default
         Gateway (192.168.149.1), traversing the reverse path symmetrically.
